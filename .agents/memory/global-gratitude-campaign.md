---
name: Global gratitude campaign
description: Founder intent for Thanks the World, its distinction from Thanks Emirates, and the moderation boundary for stories.
---

Thanks the World is a global gratitude-story campaign; Thanks Emirates remains a distinct UAE-focused initiative. Gratitude is voluntary, not a system for political endorsements or compulsory praise of leaders. Browsing must not require nationality disclosure.

The Founder now requires a real text/video contribution campaign from existing
member accounts, with pre-publication Founder review, member status management,
privacy-aware profile integration and genuine engagement. A visual preview is
not an acceptable substitute for that requested functional module.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly corrected the earlier preview-only scope while
retaining a mandatory stop for approval whenever persistence changes are needed.

**How to apply:** Inspect and reuse the existing infrastructure first. Present
the exact schema, authorization, private-media isolation, moderation and rollback
proposal before applying any migration. Do not advance past this approval gate
or label the existing preview as the finished campaign.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly requested an international expansion of gratitude while preserving the UAE initiative and avoiding political endorsement or disclosure requirements.

**How to apply:** Distinguish campaign concepts from live contribution capabilities and from curated public-figure directories. Do not present the existing regional impact directory as an operational worldwide story-submission portal.

Worldwide stories require validation and moderation before public publication, approved images, publication consent and privacy preferences. A signed-in member testimony is not equivalent to a moderated public campaign contribution.

**Why:** The existing UAE testimony flow creates a member-network post. Reusing it as worldwide public campaign publication would bypass the Founder's explicit moderation and privacy requirements.

**How to apply:** Preserve the member testimony workflow separately; do not silently redirect worldwide campaign submissions into it. Obtain the required schema approval before introducing persistent global contributions.

Regional identities are not automatically official active campaigns. Engagement
rankings and featured stories are discovery aids, not prize winners; award rules,
eligibility periods and prizes remain for the Founder to announce separately.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly reserves activation and award announcements and
requires distinct regional cultural identities within a worldwide campaign.

**How to apply:** Do not seed active regional campaigns, announce winners or
present selected countries as verified nationality. Keep unrelated regions free
of reused African Kuba ornament.

The Founder explicitly authorized the official Thanks the World campaign's
development activation, but not activation of additional regional campaigns or
any production change.

**Why:** The Founder requires the global campaign to be genuinely open in
development, not merely labelled open in the interface.

**How to apply:** Keep activation distinct from publication: an active campaign
accepts member submissions, but every contribution still requires review,
consent and eligible privacy settings before becoming public.

The Emirates campaign must retain its approved flags, dunes artwork and opening
composition. Present THANKS EMIRATES, SHUKRAN EMIRATES and شكراً الإمارات
harmoniously, with genuine RTL Arabic, not character-by-character decoration.
The Founder reviews this experience primarily on an iPhone.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly approved the introduction and requested its
refinement, not replacement, while correcting long portrait and information lists.

**How to apply:** Keep leadership a compact, optional discovery chapter with all
seven Emirates accessible; human connection and free participation remain central.
Move supporting material into accessible disclosures instead of deleting it.
Exception: remove the three repetitive “The Emirates Story” blocks (“Seven
Emirates. One shared vision.”, “Many cultures. One daily life.”, “Room to build.
Room to belong.”) completely, not into disclosures or replacement paragraphs.
**Why:** The Founder explicitly requested a shorter Emirates page while preserving
the earlier interactive federation/leadership and later campaign/participation.
**How to apply:** Do not restore these blocks during future visual refinements.
Preserve portrait provenance and distinguish official biographical sources from
the actual source of each photograph; inclusion never implies endorsement.
Use compact continent navigation that distinguishes configured active campaigns
from editorial directories. Never silently turn private Emirates member-network
testimonies into publicly approved worldwide campaign contributions.

The Founder rejected the schematic locator, abstract continent blobs, symbolic
grids/circles and visible "schematic / not official / not to scale" explanations
on the Emirates page. This page is intended for cultural, diplomatic and government
presentation, not a conceptual prototype.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly requested credible, premium visual storytelling,
clean complete ruler portraits and actual world imagery rather than a cosmetic
recolour of placeholder diagrams.

**How to apply:** Use real geographic geometry for the UAE locator and one selected
capital highlight, authentic licensed architecture/heritage imagery and the
supplied world artwork. Keep sources/licences available without cluttering the
main narrative. Preserve full portrait content within consistent natural-ratio
frames. Follow the requested narrative: introduction, federation, leadership,
shared culture, UAE global bridge, continent gateways, philosophy/participation.
Continent details should be progressively disclosed, not six simultaneous lists.
