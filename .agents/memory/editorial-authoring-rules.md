---
name: Founder editorial authoring
description: Owner requirements for multilingual page control, media proportions and video uploads.
---

The Founder expects to coordinate public editorial page text without editing source code, across all seven site languages. They specifically require adding Tropik articles, reels in Story in Motion and videos from previous Fashion Week events.

**Why:** The owner reported that adding content was possible but modifying existing page text and reaching the relevant media tools was not.

**How to apply:** Include new public editorial surfaces in the Founder editing experience and make their controls discoverable. Preserve draft review and explicit publication; public editorial control must not weaken member privacy, authentication or financial controls.

Uploaded editorial images must preserve their original proportions: landscape, square and portrait. Do not force every cover into a portrait crop.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested that the displayed image remain as uploaded and fit the magazine's design without losing its composition.

**How to apply:** Use natural-ratio images for covers and article media; constrained readers may contain the entire image but must not crop it. Distinguish editorial images from intentionally decorative backgrounds.

The requested maximum is 500 MiB per video, not unlimited storage or unlimited daily uploads.

**Why:** The owner selected “500 Mo par vidéo” to make ecosystem publishing less restrictive during trials.

**How to apply:** Keep client and server limits consistent, upload directly to storage, stream playback rather than buffering a whole large file, and retain ownership, file-type and abuse controls.
