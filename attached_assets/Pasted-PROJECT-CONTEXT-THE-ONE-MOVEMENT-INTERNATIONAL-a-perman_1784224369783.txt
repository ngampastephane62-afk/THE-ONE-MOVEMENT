PROJECT CONTEXT

THE ONE MOVEMENT INTERNATIONAL — a permanent global ecosystem connecting culture, business, investment, diplomacy, innovation, education, tourism, media and humanitarian impact. It grew from THE ONE FASHION WEEK, a fashion-for-peace event born in the Democratic Republic of Congo (editions in Bukavu, Goma and Kinshasa) whose 5th edition was publicly supported by the UN mission MONUSCO under the theme "Peace and Unity". The Movement is the evolution: no longer an event — a permanent global community. Slogan: "ONE WORLD. ONE PURPOSE. ONE MOVEMENT." Bilingual signature: "WE ARE ONE — نحن واحد".
