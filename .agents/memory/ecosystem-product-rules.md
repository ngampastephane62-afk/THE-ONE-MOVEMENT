name: Ecosystem membership and editorial regions
description: Stakeholder's monthly professional-network, verification, media and regional tribute requirements.
---

Permanent TOMI membership is free. The Founder says “WE DO NOT SELL BELONGING.” Revenue should primarily come from optional services, not compulsory membership subscriptions. Humanitarian giving remains separate.

**Why:** The Founder's Global Universe Experience mandate supersedes the earlier monthly-membership direction.

**How to apply:** The existing US$29 and US$99 monthly prices are NOT approved commercial pricing. Do not change pricing or Stripe without separate authorization. A possible ten-day trial concerns selected optional premium tools only, subject to later approval; it must not limit permanent free membership. Paid promotion must be labelled and cannot buy certification, institutional credibility or guaranteed programme participation.

Each event has its own income-generating professional participation services; participants need not buy a sponsor package. The owner sets the non-sponsor Summit speaker fee at US$10,000. Designers/stylists, models, hair stylists and makeup artists also need distinct service offers, but their fees have not been provided.

**Why:** The owner explicitly distinguishes impact, speaking, photo/video, branding, highlights, media opportunities and networking from purchasing sponsorship.

**How to apply:** Keep programme-specific services separate from annual sponsorship, monthly subscriptions and capital investment. Existing sponsors should have their written inclusions checked before an additional fee is proposed. Do not invent role prices, promise editorial coverage or assume all sponsor levels guarantee a speaking slot.

Earlier requests to enable real monthly subscriptions do not authorize launching that model under the newer free-membership mandate.

**Why:** The Founder now explicitly separates free belonging from optional paid services and rejects the existing monthly prices as approved pricing.

**How to apply:** Wait for separate commercial and implementation approval. Never treat sandbox payments as real money or proof of launch readiness.

After Join the Movement, members should immediately reach an editable account/profile. Inviting ten people must NOT be mandatory for ordinary publication. Invitations may form part of future optional contribution goals; rewards require separate Founder approval. The earlier ten-person business-attestation rule is a distinct requirement, not silently repealed.

**Why:** The Founder's newer mandate explicitly rejects mandatory invitations for ordinary publication and requests optional meaningful contribution and recognition instead.

**How to apply:** Ordinary publication must not depend on recruitment. Keep invitation tracking and records; do not introduce rewards without separate approval. Removing the ordinary-publication invitation condition does not authorize weakening existing specialized commercial access controls, changing prices, activating trials or modifying Stripe. Prevent fake accounts and artificial engagement. Attestations remain supporting evidence, not proof of legal registration.

Videos should retain their native aspect ratio, support member comments and likes, and offer sharing to social networks. Thanks Emirates should use a UAE flag, classical animation and revised typography, featuring multiple emirates including Abu Dhabi rather than Dubai alone.

**Why:** The stakeholder supplied additional portrait videos and requested social amplification and inclusive UAE representation.

**How to apply:** Avoid forced landscape crops and respect reduced-motion settings. Distinguish user-initiated sharing from automatic posting to a connected social account.

Members should be able to list Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn profiles. The stakeholder also wants the possibility of republishing a member's own Instagram post on TOMI.

**Why:** They requested profile links and separately asked whether a post published on Instagram could also be published on the member's TOMI account.

**How to apply:** Treat a profile URL, an authorised account connection and a replicated post as separate capabilities. Preserve member choice and TOMI publishing eligibility; never describe an entered URL as a connected account or automatically copy content without authorisation.

Member discovery should use explicit saved sector choices, while keeping all 17 official sectors accessible. With no chosen sector, preserve general discovery. Profile completion is optional; discovery must not introduce mandatory onboarding or expose private preferences.

**Why:** The Founder authorized targeted welcome and discovery improvements, not inferred-interest personalization, a recommendation algorithm or a second member identity.

**How to apply:** Reuse existing programmes, resources and people discovery. Do not imply that the “For You” feed is algorithmically personalized, bookmarks register participation, programme links guarantee admission, or activation unlocks all messaging. Subscription and messaging eligibility changes require separate authorization.

The One Awards should include every officially established ambassador, with sourced biographies and official social links. Separate regional impact-leader selections (Africa, USA and eventually other regions/sectors) from TOMI ambassadors.

The same peace-ambassador nominees are announced as planned Summit speakers and Fashion Week red-carpet participants, as well as participants in other activities. Use one shared profile across programmes, not separate contradictory biographies.

**Why:** The stakeholder explicitly clarified these cross-programme roles. A nomination is not an award already won, and an announcement is not proof of past attendance.

**How to apply:** Preserve nominee and planned-participation wording; source professional work and businesses separately from TOMI's own announcement.

**Why:** The stakeholder asks for privileged ambassador profiles, collaboration enquiries and six notable figures per sector, citing Mandela, Dangote and the Obamas as editorial examples.

**How to apply:** Attribute and date factual sources; Google and Wikipedia are discovery aids, not proof of TOMI affiliation. Do not imply deceased leaders are available for collaboration, invent private contacts, or label a curated selection an authoritative global ranking.
