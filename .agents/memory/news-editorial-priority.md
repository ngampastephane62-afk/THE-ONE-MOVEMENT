---
name: News editorial priority
description: Founder rules for current news, publication ordering, archive separation and cinematic presentation.
---

News leads with the most recently published CMS article, not historical Fashion
Week coverage. The editorial order is latest, featured announcements, discovery,
videos/reels/interviews, programme/event/opportunity updates, then the historical
press archive. Keep a story in one editorial group rather than duplicating it.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly wants News to tell TOMI's present while preserving
its attributed history.

**How to apply:** Use the public published snapshot and CMS publication timestamp,
never an event date or a draft. Keep approved static editorial media source-labelled
and separate from the latest CMS hero. Preserve archive records, original uncropped
captures, attribution, filters, links, engagement and Founder management.

The supplied animation references require coordinated media masks, restrained
scale, grouped headline reveals and lateral editorial compositions, not just
generic fades. Mobile readability and natural scrolling take priority.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly said earlier animations did not sufficiently
reflect the reference and requested a motion specification before implementation.

**How to apply:** Choreograph actual content after it loads; allow immediate
navigation and static/reduced-motion states. Do not invent missing types, dates,
articles or engagement, or change backend/schema/auth/payment/storage to fill a
visual section. This mandate is frontend-only and does not authorize deployment.
