---
name: Member social network and official certifications
description: Founder requirements for the member universe, event-specific status, humanitarian donations and programme packages.
---

The Founder wants signing in after membership to open a distinct social-network universe, similar to LinkedIn, not merely a profile-and-bookmarks page. Members should upload business media, publish posts and reels, follow each other, discover profiles inside and outside their sectors, see likes and views, and start private discussions.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly described the existing member workspace as insufficient for launching a community where people subscribe, post and generate traffic.

**How to apply:** Extend the existing member product and authentication rather than treating a redesigned profile page or a static feed as completion. Preserve the approved public homepage.

The member network must retain visible The One Movement International branding while scrolling. Comment authors should be identifiable through linked profiles and their member descriptions so members can discover and contact each other.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly requested persistent branding and visible descriptions on comments to facilitate connections between members.

**How to apply:** Keep the brand accessible on mobile as well as desktop; show descriptions members actually entered, never invented biographies, and preserve consent-based messaging.

New members may join, create a limited profile, follow people, like and comment after their email is verified; they do not wait for individual CEO approval or buy an event package to access the social network. To create their own posts, ordinary members must first recruit more than 10 people (at least 11) who create accounts and follow their profile. Official certifications remain a separate approval process.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly selected access after email verification rather than manual approval of every new member, then clarified the 11-person referral threshold for creating posts, without requiring event participation. Existing Founder content must remain possible.

**How to apply:** Do not introduce an unrequested membership-approval queue or confuse email verification with business certification. Enforce posting eligibility on the server using distinct, genuinely new accounts that both attributed the invitation and still follow the inviter. Free membership, profile creation, discovery, likes and comments remain available before posting unlock; packages and events are separate.

Verified users must be able to explore the feed immediately after signing in, before filling in their profile. Profession, sectors, biography and other details can be completed later. Members should easily open their followers and following lists.

**Why:** The Founder relayed user feedback that the compulsory profile form caused visitors to leave before seeing the platform.

**How to apply:** Use progressive onboarding without a blocking profile form. Preserve explicit consent before making a profile discoverable to other members; do not confuse this with optional public-internet visibility. Never promise that Google or a new-device security check can always be skipped.

Email must be the primary registration and sign-in option, accepting any valid provider, including iCloud and professional addresses. Google is optional, not required.

**Why:** The Founder clarified that members should use their existing email to avoid extra procedures and must not perceive the service as Gmail-only. This supersedes the earlier Google-first presentation request.

**How to apply:** Keep email fields before social sign-in options and clearly distinguish creating an account from signing into an existing one. Do not remove email verification or bypass account security to reduce steps.

“Premium” registration means a polished, Instagram-like experience that remains free, not a paid subscription. Every member should have a personal profile page, with their cover behind the profile presentation and owner-controlled layout choices.

**Why:** The stakeholder explicitly chose “Inscription gratuite, expérience haut de gamme” when asked to distinguish visual quality from paid membership.

**How to apply:** Keep registration and profile appearance customization free, optional and separate from paid programme packages. Preserve existing public-profile and public-media consent; a personal page does not mean automatic publication on the public internet.

Members may choose to make their profile publicly visible outside the signed-in network; private contact details, messages and previously member-only posts are not implicitly made public.

**Why:** The Founder asked to make member profiles public and shareable, but existing members consented only to signed-in network visibility for their activity. The older public-directory opt-in covered text fields, not network photos or covers.

**How to apply:** Honor each member's explicit public visibility setting; require separate consent before showing a network photo or cover publicly. Keep anonymous profile data limited and do not expose message or post media merely because a profile is public. Public profile responses should not remain cached after an opt-out.

Distinguish ordinary ecosystem membership from official event/programme participation. Partner, sponsor, media partner and official-model certifications must be approved by the CEO and scoped to the relevant event/programme. A person calling themselves a model does not make them an official event model; account email verification does not certify a business or a partnership.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly requires his validation and wants to distinguish community members from members who have subscribed to official partnership packages.

**How to apply:** Provide genuine approval controls, not self-assigned badges. Identify the Founder through a confirmed authenticated account; do not infer privileged access from a self-entered profession or screenshots. Founder messaging should respect access controls.

Programme/event packages differ by participation role, including partner, exhibitor and speaker. The Founder will supply downloadable package documents; do not invent their prices, benefits or approval terms. Participation should not terminate continued ecosystem access.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly described distinct packages per sector, event and programme and continued membership benefits afterward.

**How to apply:** Support programme-specific documents, applications and official status independently of general community membership.

The Founder requests humanitarian actions and an actual Donate pathway supporting people affected by the crisis in eastern Democratic Republic of Congo.

**Why:** The product is also intended to enable humanitarian impact, not only professional networking.

**How to apply:** Confirm the legal recipient and connect real payment processing before claiming donations are active. Do not invent beneficiaries, collected totals, impact results or tax-deductibility.

The Founder identifies The One Movement International as the collecting organization, collaborating according to the humanitarian emergency with Fondation Panzi, Juhudi Fondation, MONUSCO, UNICEF or UNHCR.

**Why:** This is the Founder's answer about the donation recipient and collaborators; the beneficiary varies by emergency.

**How to apply:** Define the beneficiary for each actual campaign, not a blanket promise that every named organization receives every donation. Merchant eligibility and legal fundraising requirements still need to be satisfied before payment activation.

The Founder provided a Panzi Foundation fundraising film and a humanitarian story produced with “Fauna Rev” (the film credits identify FONAREV). The Panzi appeal may lead directly to Panzi's own page after their approval.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly distinguishes Panzi's direct fundraising pathway from collecting money through TOMI, and supplied both films to support the humanitarian story.

**How to apply:** Keep authorship separate and use the on-film FONAREV credit. Do not activate a Panzi fundraising destination until their approval and the official URL are confirmed. A direct Panzi donation is received through Panzi's own service, not TOMI's Stripe checkout.
