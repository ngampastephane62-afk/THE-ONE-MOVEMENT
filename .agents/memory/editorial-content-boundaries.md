Keep Tropik's media distribution catalogue in Media and partner profiles in Partners. Historical press coverage belongs in a separate News page, not in Media or duplicated in Partners. Vision should follow the supplied narrative, use the donation film instead of the humanitarian photograph, and avoid repeated image collages or copied sections.

**Why:** The stakeholder explicitly requested that media stay in Media, partners stay in Partners, and information and images not be repeated across pages.

**How to apply:** Short conceptual descriptions and links are appropriate elsewhere, but do not duplicate the media catalogue, partner galleries or entire report sections.

The stakeholder describes a ten-year journey and confirms that the supplied press outlets are longstanding partners with evidence. Their explicit News requirement is publisher attribution, publisher logos, links to the outlets' pages and an administrator's ability to add more coverage.

**Why:** The stakeholder corrected an initial plan to put the press review in Media and supplied screenshots documenting the coverage. Treat this documented coverage confidently without repeating speculative or “unverified partner” labels.

**How to apply:** Group consecutive screenshots of the same article into one publication. Preserve their historical context rather than turning an old programme announcement into a current event or a screenshot's social count into total reach. Link to an exact article only when its URL is established; label publisher-home/profile links honestly.

The stakeholder describes the supplied donation film as assistance after the fifth edition of The One Movement to people displaced by war in Goma, North Kivu, DRC, in collaboration with UNHCR.

**Why:** This is the stakeholder's supplied documentary context, not a claim of fundraising totals, current fundraising authorization or blanket UN endorsement.

**How to apply:** Use this context in the film caption; do not imply that every humanitarian archive film is the same operation.

Thanks Emirates is TOMI's gratitude, coexistence and personal-story initiative, also titled “Thank You, Emirates — We Are One” in the supplied strategic document. People should be able to join and share their own written/video testimonies.

**Why:** The stakeholder requested participation and video submissions. The source document labels 300 stories as a target, hypothetical quotes as illustrative, and institutional engagements and recognition as subject to approval.

**How to apply:** Never publish example quotes as authentic testimonials, target counts as achieved outcomes, or imply UAE governmental recognition or Emirates airline sponsorship. Explain the actual visibility of submitted content before submission.

The stakeholder wants a Dubai documentary announcement and links to CANAL+. The narrative distinguishes previous CANAL+/TV5MONDE broadcasts from a future documentary project subject to final confirmation.

**Why:** A previous broadcast or media relationship does not establish acquisition or a scheduled broadcast of the Dubai production.

**How to apply:** Clearly distinguish announced development and intended distribution from confirmed commissioning or scheduling. Broadcaster-wide audience statistics must have a dated official source and must never be presented as views of TOMI's interview.

The documentary section must represent TOMI's ongoing documentary history, with Goma first and Kinshasa subsequently, not only a future Dubai film. The stakeholder also identifies a supplied trailer as produced by MONUSCO through Radio Okapi.

**Why:** The stakeholder states they have made documentary videos since the beginning and wants highlights leading to longer exclusive content.

**How to apply:** Distinguish TOMI productions, externally produced trailers and the future Dubai project. Credit MONUSCO/Radio Okapi accurately; do not assume permission to place third-party footage behind a paid-access restriction merely because it was supplied.

Humanitarian contributions are received by The One Movement International, which chooses allocation according to urgency. Do not restore a confusing Panzi-direct/TOMI donation split or promise a fixed earmark. The stakeholder names Gaza, Goma, Bukavu, Ukraine and people suffering anywhere.

**Why:** The stakeholder explicitly corrected the beneficiary and allocation policy. Their priority is funding schools and children's education centres for displaced people, orphans, people with disabilities, people with albinism and rape survivors; these are funding goals, not completed construction.

**How to apply:** Separate official crisis information from TOMI's documented actions. Accept offers of goods, equipment and logistics through coordinated contact, not unsolicited shipment. Payment integration is not evidence of fundraising authorization: real collection still requires explicit approval and eligibility checks.

Humanitarian actions and films are free to access, with voluntary contributions entirely separate from professional ecosystem subscriptions. Do not restore the earlier US$10 humanitarian-film purchase.

**Why:** On 2026-10-16 the stakeholder clarified that the paid product is a monthly professional/social ecosystem subscription, not humanitarian action, superseding the earlier film-price instruction. They supplied distinct TOMI Goma and MONUSCO/Radio Okapi files.

**How to apply:** Keep both supplied humanitarian films public. Goma is about 59 seconds, not feature-length. Kinshasa footage has not been supplied; Dubai is a separate future project. Never describe test contributions as real fundraising.

There is no TOMI fundraising ceiling; the stakeholder explicitly gives a US$1 million contribution as an example.

**Why:** The stakeholder rejected an application-imposed maximum because emergencies can require substantial funds.

**How to apply:** Separate processor/card limits from willingness to accept contributions. Route major gifts into persisted financial offers for eligibility, due diligence and coordinated payment arrangements; an offer is not money received. Do not invent bank details or bypass fundraising authorization.

The international-language scope covers the existing site, not only Thanks Emirates or the donation page: English, French, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, Swahili and simplified Chinese, with Arabic right-to-left layout.

**Why:** The stakeholder requested “langues internationales” for the whole site; these seven languages were the committed scope.

**How to apply:** Keep the selector persistent across navigation and translate shared UI and form instructions. Preserve user-authored content, names, source attribution and exact monetary terms; do not send visitor content to a live translation service.
