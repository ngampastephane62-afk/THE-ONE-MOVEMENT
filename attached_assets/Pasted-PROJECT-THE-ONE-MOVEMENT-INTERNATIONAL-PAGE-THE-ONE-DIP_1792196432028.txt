STRATEGIC CONCEPT UPDATE

We are refining the concept of The One Diplomatic Gala.

The current page does not adequately communicate the actual vision.
Do not simply shorten the existing text or improve its grammar.

The Gala is intended to be an international, community-centred
experience where participating countries can present their cultures,
communities, businesses, creative talent, traditions and innovations.

The central concept is:

THE WORLD, UNDER ONE ROOF.

MANY NATIONS. MANY CULTURES. ONE SHARED FUTURE.

This event must be visually and conceptually distinct from
The One Fashion Week International.

The One Fashion Week focuses on fashion, designers, collections,
models and the fashion industry.

The One Diplomatic Gala focuses on national representation,
cultural exchange, communities, diplomacy, business, innovation,
humanitarian engagement and international collaboration.
