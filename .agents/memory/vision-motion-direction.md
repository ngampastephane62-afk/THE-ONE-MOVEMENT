For Our Vision and Thanks the World, all decorative maps, gold connections,
particles, flags and patterns must stay behind readable copy and buttons.
Use isolated surfaces with background 0, decoration 1, readability wash 2 and
content 3; decoration must ignore pointer events. Avoid oversized glowing dots.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly corrected gold connections crossing in front of
the text and repeated this restriction with the supplied reference artwork.

**How to apply:** Preserve this hierarchy when changing hero or scroll motion,
including nested stacking contexts and pseudo-elements. Verify pointer behaviour,
mobile readability, reduced motion and offscreen pausing.

The Founder-provided golden world images are the authoritative main hero
artwork, not placeholders to replace with stock or a generated generic map.
The Museum of Ancient Art clip is a motion reference only: use original TOMI
horizontal artwork movement, layered depth and staged typography, never museum
assets or branding. Preserve the existing Goma film and historical context.

**Why:** The Founder expressly assigned one supplied map to each hero and asked
for an original adaptation, not copying the museum or rebuilding the website.

**How to apply:** Derivatives may optimize the supplied images without changing
their identity. Keep the homepage, authentication, membership and payment
configuration outside this refinement's scope.
