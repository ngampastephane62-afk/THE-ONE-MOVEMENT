THE ONE MOVEMENT INTERNATIONAL (TOMI)
PHASE 2 — TASK 8

COMMUNITY MESSAGING ACCESS
FREE CONNECTION & MEMBER SAFETY AUDIT

FOUNDER AUTHORIZATION:
READ-ONLY TECHNICAL AND PRODUCT AUDIT

IMPLEMENTATION: NOT AUTHORIZED

==================================================
1. FOUNDER'S VISION
==================================================

TOMI exists to connect people, cultures,
communities, institutions and opportunities.

Membership is permanently free.

Ordinary human connection should not depend
on purchasing a monthly subscription.

The Founder is considering making ordinary
member-to-member messaging freely accessible,
subject to appropriate safety protections.

This is a proposed policy requiring final
Founder approval before implementation.

==================================================
2. AUDIT OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Inspect the existing messaging architecture.

Identify exactly where subscriptions,
membership status and other eligibility
conditions affect:

- Starting a conversation
- Accepting conversation requests
- Replying to messages
- Viewing existing conversations
- Sending attachments
- Blocking members
- Reporting abuse
- Professional or business messaging

Do not modify any access rules.

==================================================
3. EXISTING COMMUNICATION FLOW
==================================================

Document the current journey:

1. Discover a member.
2. Open their profile.
3. Request a conversation.
4. Accept or reject the request.
5. Exchange messages.
6. Block or report another member.

For each step identify:

- Frontend components
- Backend handlers
- Database dependencies
- Existing permission checks
- Subscription requirements
- Safety controls

Distinguish code behavior from
runtime-verified behavior.

==================================================
4. PROPOSED FREE COMMUNITY MESSAGING
==================================================

Evaluate a possible future model:

A. Free ordinary member messaging

Eligible members can communicate
without a paid subscription.

B. Consent-based first contact

Where appropriate, new contacts begin
with a conversation request.

C. Member protection

Preserve:

- Authentication
- Explicit network activation
- Account verification
- Blocking
- Reporting
- Suspension restrictions
- Rate limits
- Anti-spam controls
- Privacy settings

D. Specialized commercial messaging

Future advanced professional messaging,
promotional campaigns or mass outreach
may remain separate paid services.

Do not create those services.

==================================================
5. SAFETY AND ABUSE ANALYSIS
==================================================

Assess risks involving:

- Spam
- Harassment
- Unsolicited commercial messages
- Fake accounts
- Repeated rejected requests
- Mass outreach
- Privacy violations
- Suspended members

Identify protections already present
and protections that are missing.

Do not weaken existing protections.

==================================================
6. SUBSCRIPTION DEPENDENCIES
==================================================

Locate every subscription requirement
directly connected to messaging.

Explain whether removing the ordinary
conversation paywall would affect:

- Other network features
- Business publications
- Reels
- Professional services
- Stripe
- Existing subscription records

Do not modify subscriptions or Stripe.

==================================================

==================================================

Return:

1. Current messaging architecture

4. Missing safety protections
5. Recommended free messaging policy

8. Backend files potentially affected

10. Tests required
11. Risks and dependencies
12. Decisions requiring Founder approval

==================================================

- No dependency installation
- No production member data access
- No authentication changes
- No interference with other agents

READ-ONLY STATIC ANALYSIS ONLY.
