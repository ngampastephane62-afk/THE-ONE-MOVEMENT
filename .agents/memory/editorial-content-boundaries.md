Keep Tropik's media distribution catalogue in Media and partner profiles in Partners. Historical press coverage belongs in a separate News page, not in Media or duplicated in Partners. Vision should follow the supplied narrative, use the donation film instead of the humanitarian photograph, and avoid repeated image collages or copied sections.

**Why:** The stakeholder explicitly requested that media stay in Media, partners stay in Partners, and information and images not be repeated across pages.

**How to apply:** Short conceptual descriptions and links are appropriate elsewhere, but do not duplicate the media catalogue, partner galleries or entire report sections.

The stakeholder describes a ten-year journey and confirms that the supplied press outlets are longstanding partners with evidence. Their explicit News requirement is publisher attribution, publisher logos, links to the outlets' pages and an administrator's ability to add more coverage.

**Why:** The stakeholder corrected an initial plan to put the press review in Media and supplied screenshots documenting the coverage. Treat this documented coverage confidently without repeating speculative or “unverified partner” labels.

**How to apply:** Group consecutive screenshots of the same article into one publication. Preserve their historical context rather than turning an old programme announcement into a current event or a screenshot's social count into total reach. Link to an exact article only when its URL is established; label publisher-home/profile links honestly.

The stakeholder describes the supplied donation film as assistance after the fifth edition of The One Movement to people displaced by war in Goma, North Kivu, DRC, in collaboration with UNHCR.

**Why:** This is the stakeholder's supplied documentary context, not a claim of fundraising totals, current fundraising authorization or blanket UN endorsement.

**How to apply:** Use this context in the film caption; do not imply that every humanitarian archive film is the same operation.

Thanks Emirates is TOMI's gratitude, coexistence and personal-story initiative, also titled “Thank You, Emirates — We Are One” in the supplied strategic document. People should be able to join and share their own written/video testimonies.

**Why:** The stakeholder requested participation and video submissions. The source document labels 300 stories as a target, hypothetical quotes as illustrative, and institutional engagements and recognition as subject to approval.

**How to apply:** Never publish example quotes as authentic testimonials, target counts as achieved outcomes, or imply UAE governmental recognition or Emirates airline sponsorship. Explain the actual visibility of submitted content before submission.

The stakeholder wants a Dubai documentary announcement and links to CANAL+. The narrative distinguishes previous CANAL+/TV5MONDE broadcasts from a future documentary project subject to final confirmation.

**Why:** A previous broadcast or media relationship does not establish acquisition or a scheduled broadcast of the Dubai production.

**How to apply:** Clearly distinguish announced development and intended distribution from confirmed commissioning or scheduling. Broadcaster-wide audience statistics must have a dated official source and must never be presented as views of TOMI's interview.
