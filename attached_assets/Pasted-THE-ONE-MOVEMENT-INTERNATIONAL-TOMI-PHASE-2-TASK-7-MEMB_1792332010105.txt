not simply a social media feed.

--------------------------------------------------

Inspect the existing member entry experience.

- ActivationPrompt
- CompleteProfile
- PendingSector
- PendingInvitation
- Workspace
- ProgrammeLink
- ResourceCard

Improve the clarity and hierarchy of
existing welcome messages.

I BELONG.
I CAN CONNECT.
I CAN CONTRIBUTE.
I CAN GROW.

and existing explicit sector preferences.

to general discovery.

Do not create artificial recommendations.

Do not expose private preferences.
