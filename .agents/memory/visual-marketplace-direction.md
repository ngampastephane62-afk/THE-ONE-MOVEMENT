---
name: TOMI visual and marketplace direction
description: Stakeholder direction for site identity, regional illustration, programme covers and the proposed commercial marketplace.
---

The stakeholder wants an African identity inspired by Wakanda and Kuba textiles from DRC across the ecosystem, with the actual TOMI logo in the header instead of a typed wordmark. Use the supplied green/gold Emirates skyline artwork with motion; Africa should feature African monuments including pyramids, and Europe the Eiffel Tower. Illustrative regional composites must not masquerade as photographs.

**Why:** The stakeholder supplied the backgrounds and explicitly corrected the header and region-specific visual direction.

**How to apply:** Keep patterns readable, motion pausable and reduced-motion aware. Do not imply Marvel affiliation or historical authenticity for original geometric artwork.

The stakeholder designates the supplied Naomi Campbell artwork as The One Awards cover, and the supplied three-model artwork as The One Fashion Week cover.

**Why:** They explicitly corrected which artwork represents each programme, despite wording printed on the posters.

**How to apply:** Keep programme-cover choice separate from any claim about celebrity attendance, casting or awards already conferred.

The stakeholder identifies Dr Denis Mukwege as a TOMI partner and requests certification. The meaning is TOMI's own declared partner recognition, not a verified member account, UN endorsement or Nobel Committee certification.

**Why:** Partner recognition, Nobel laureate status and account verification are different claims.

**How to apply:** Label issuer and scope of badges explicitly. Use the Nobel Committee as the source of the 2018 award.

The stakeholder proposes a multi-brand marketplace for jewellery, accessories and other products, each contracted brand having its own product space, with potentially 5% of sales supporting TOMI humanitarian action.

**Why:** Their stated goal is to let people buy and sell while helping humanity.

The stakeholder has chosen TOMI's own commission as the source of the humanitarian contribution, rather than an additional deduction funded by the seller or a buyer surcharge.

**Why:** They explicitly selected “TOMI, prélevés sur sa propre commission”.

**How to apply:** The marketplace is not activated. The calculation base (5% of sales funded from TOMI's commission versus 5% of the commission itself) and total commission rate still need explicit clarification, along with refunds, fulfilment and authorisations before enabling payments or claiming transfers. Humanitarian giving stays separate from professional subscriptions.

Marketplace sellers and buyers should manage transactions through their own profiles. The stakeholder wants a stylist's subscription to enable product listings, with sellers receiving proceeds in their own bank accounts and uploading a business licence to qualify.

**Why:** They explicitly describe this as the benefit that makes a monthly subscription attractive to brands and stylists, not a TOMI-managed retail catalogue.

**How to apply:** Design seller product/order management and buyer purchase tracking as real account capabilities. Keep business-licence documents private and review them separately from paid membership and community attestations. Bank onboarding belongs with the payment provider, not public profiles or chat. Their phrase “souscrit pendant un an” still needs clarification: do not impose a twelve-month waiting period or invent an annual prepaid plan without confirmation.
