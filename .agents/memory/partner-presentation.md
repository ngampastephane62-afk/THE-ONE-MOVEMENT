name: Partner presentation hierarchy
description: Owner-required priority, historical trust, banking and media audience distinctions.
---

Lead the Partners page with major institutional, governmental and diplomatic relationships, then banking/private companies, then international brands. Broadcasters, press and other media come last, followed by their audience statistics.

**Why:** The owner explicitly rejected the media-first logo inventory. A prospective partner must see the Movement’s established institutional and commercial relationships before media coverage to understand its history and trustworthiness.

**How to apply:** This order applies to the entire page, not merely the logo category headings. Documentary/broadcast features must not precede the institutional partners again. Show Rawbank distinctly as a banking relationship; the owner will add other banks progressively. Do not invent additional banks, sponsorship tiers or continuous ten-year contracts from a decade of historical relationships.

Give each official-meeting photograph three seconds of actual visibility; retain the current image until the next has loaded.

**Why:** The owner specifically corrected the earlier one-second instruction after seeing empty photo frames and pictures moving too quickly.

**How to apply:** Start the initial timer only after the first photo loads as well. Preserve manual navigation, pause and reduced-motion support. Present logos in consistent, legible slides rather than oversized clipped table cells.

Public broadcaster audience figures describe the publisher’s overall scale, not measured exposure of the Movement’s content.

**Why:** The owner requests figures for every media partner, but the available sources use incompatible measures: subscribers, weekly contacts, viewers and distribution households.

**How to apply:** Label each metric and source period separately and never sum them. An unavailable channel-specific figure is not zero. Do not reuse Africa24 TV’s distribution footprint for Africa24 Infinity.

Partner leadership introductions must use a TOMI-handled form only; never expose executives’ email addresses or route visitors directly to them.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose “Aucune adresse : uniquement le formulaire de mise en relation” and wants enquiries to arrive with TOMI.

**How to apply:** Keep the partner target separate from the requester’s own organisation. Make requests accessible to the Founder, explain that receipt does not guarantee an introduction, and use verified public names and correct institutional roles. Mark unknown leadership honestly; distinguish parent-group executives from individual brand or local management.
