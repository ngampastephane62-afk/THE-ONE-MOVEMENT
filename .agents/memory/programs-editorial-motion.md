---
name: Programs editorial motion
description: Founder constraints for cinematic refinements of the six official Programs.
---

Programme pages must keep event-specific information, archives and relevant applications. General ecosystem pillars, sector directories, knowledge/learning material and cross-sector collaboration belong in the Ecosystem or the relevant sector, not inside programmes.

**Why:** The Founder repeated this correction after screenshots still showed “Six ways in”, “Keep learning and connecting between editions”, “Knowledge organised by sector” and “Beyond one sector” in programme pages.

**How to apply:** Move the actual blocks and their working destinations; adding a link elsewhere while leaving the generic content in the programme is not enough. Preserve the existing designer/model/photographer and other relevant event applications. Remove obsolete navigation anchors and avoid duplicate copies at the destination.

The latest Gala correction asks to restore the former hall hero instead of the audience photograph, and to keep its two conceptual cultural illustrations without overlap.

**Why:** The Founder corrected the previous hero replacement and identified illustrations covering one another and their captions on mobile.

**How to apply:** Keep concept imagery explicitly labelled as illustration, never as a confirmed upcoming event photograph. Preserve original archive files when changing their placement.

Programs animation upgrades must preserve the six official programmes, approved
names and facts, selected Founder photographs and existing destinations.
The supplied immersive clips are motion references only, not replacement
content or videos to embed on the page.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly requested an animation upgrade of the existing
Programs page, not a content rewrite or replacement of the selected photography.

**How to apply:** Adapt staged typography, masked image entry, depth and subtle
atmosphere to TOMI's teal/gold/ivory identity. Keep mobile reading and the numeral,
title, keywords and description hierarchy clear. Avoid neon, heavy animation,
hidden fallback content or decoration in front of text. Apply the background
layering rule in [Vision motion direction](vision-motion-direction.md).

Keep each programme's real like, comments and share controls on one row, with
additional sharing choices below and outside the programme link.

**Why:** The Founder called out mobile spacing and engagement layout as explicit
requirements alongside the cinematic presentation.

**How to apply:** Refine page-scoped presentation without changing engagement
contracts, counts, authentication, programme details or other pages.

Programme pages should use fewer visible elements and concise explanations,
while sharing an adaptable editorial structure and retaining distinct identities.
The later six-programme brief permits consolidating repetitive descriptions,
not merely animating the original paragraphs unchanged.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly requested “LESS CLUTTER. STRONGER STORYTELLING.
BETTER INFORMATION. MORE MEANINGFUL INTERACTIONS.”

**How to apply:** Introduce each programme before administrative details. Give
Awards nominees early, compact discovery; keep Gala's cultural identity,
Summit's year-round ecosystem and Fashion Week's historical footage distinct.
Avoid empty sections and duplicating the seventeen ecosystem sectors as programmes.

Awards recognition is not for sale. Nominations and selection are independent
of sponsorship, professional services and other commercial arrangements.

**Why:** The Founder called this a critical principle of The One Awards.

**How to apply:** Keep the statement prominent; do not invent judging criteria,
sell nominations, promise selection, or infer confirmed Summit/Fashion Week
appearances from an Awards nomination. Publish confirmed programme appearances
separately. Preserve approved biography records while removing wealth estimates
from nominee presentation.

The Founder requires consistent cinematic treatment for all important public
headings, not only the programme index. This includes Media, Partners,
Humanitarian Action and each sector, with coordinated labels, titles, accents
and media rather than the same generic fade everywhere.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly repeated that static headings and inconsistent
motion across pages remain unacceptable.

**How to apply:** Reuse a shared heading/motion system, keep text immediately
readable on mobile and in reduced-motion mode, and never leave headings hidden
when an observer or an animation fails. Do not animate every paragraph letter
by letter. The later Founder correction explicitly requests a shared, very faint
white watermark on green backgrounds; sector imagery and primary motion should
still retain their own identities.
