# TOMI Future Product Requirements

These requirements are **post-launch product direction**, not current public functionality.

## Member ecosystem

- A future member's selected sector and profession should shape their profile and ecosystem experience.
- Sector examples include Fashion, Music, Dance, Film, Art, Business and other approved TOMI worlds.
- Profession taxonomies should remain reusable and expandable rather than exhaustive.
- Members may eventually discover and interact with relevant people inside their selected worlds.
- The public website taxonomy should remain compatible with that future member platform.

## Membership and monetization

- The future direction may combine low-friction ecosystem entry with paid upgrades for deeper access and additional functionality.
- No entry amount, currency, price, entitlement or package is approved for public use.
- Payments, subscriptions, wallets and billing are out of scope for the current public-website phase.
- Currency, recurring billing, cancellation, refunds, membership terms and package entitlements require business and legal approval before implementation or publication.

## Current boundary

Expression-of-interest and institutional-inquiry submissions do not create accounts, memberships, confirmed places or paid access. Public pages may describe future direction only when clearly marked as future and unconfirmed.
