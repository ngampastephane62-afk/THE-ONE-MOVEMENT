PROJECT: THE ONE MOVEMENT INTERNATIONAL
WEBSITE: THE ONE FASHION WEEK INTERNATIONAL PARTICIPATION PAGE

OBJECTIVE

Audit and completely improve the existing participation page for
The One Fashion Week International.

The current page contains vague descriptions such as "Media Participation",
"Designer / Stylist", "Model", "Hair Stylist", "Tailored Fee on Enquiry",
and repeated "Enquire" buttons.

This content does not adequately communicate our business model.

We need a premium international participation and subscription platform
where professionals and organisations can understand the event, explore
their category, compare available packages, and apply or subscribe
according to the applicable terms.

IMPORTANT:
Preserve the existing project architecture, visual identity, database,
authentication system, existing functionality and unrelated pages.
Do not rebuild the application from scratch.
Do not delete existing user records or production data.
Do not change unrelated functionality.
Inspect the existing code before making modifications.

BRAND POSITIONING

The One Fashion Week International is an international fashion platform
connecting fashion, culture, creativity, business and international
collaboration.

The website must communicate a premium, professional, international
experience.

Use the existing brand identity, including its dark teal, cream and
gold palette, elegant typography, premium spacing and refined editorial
aesthetic.

Keep the design responsive and optimise it for mobile devices.

PAGE INTRODUCTION

Title:
THE ONE FASHION WEEK INTERNATIONAL

Headline:
BE PART OF THE GLOBAL FASHION MOVEMENT.

Introduction:
More than a runway. A platform for fashion, culture, creativity,
business and international connections.

Body:
The One Fashion Week International brings together designers, models,
stylists, accessory creators, fashion professionals, media organisations,
production teams, brands and industry partners on an international
platform dedicated to fashion, culture, innovation and collaboration.

Our platform creates opportunities for professionals and organisations
to present their work, connect with industry stakeholders, build
strategic relationships and participate in an international fashion
experience.

Whether you are an established brand, an emerging designer, a professional
model, a media organisation or a member of a production team, there is a
place for you within our ecosystem.

Call to action:
CHOOSE YOUR CATEGORY. SELECT YOUR PARTICIPATION PACKAGE.
BECOME PART OF THE MOVEMENT.

Create a clear participation-category section containing:

1. Fashion Designers & Fashion Brands
2. Models & Talent
3. Accessory Designers & Creators
4. Stylists & Creative Directors
5. Hair & Makeup Professionals
6. Backstage Managers & Event Coordinators
7. Media Partners & Press
8. Photographers & Videographers
9. Technical & Production Teams
10. Logistics & Event Operations
11. Brands, Sponsors & Commercial Partners

Each category must have:
- A professional title.
- A concise, accurate description.
- Relevant potential participation benefits.
- A dedicated category page or category-specific section.
- A clear button leading to the relevant package list or application form.

Do not use generic descriptions repeated across all categories.

PACKAGE AND SUBSCRIPTION SYSTEM

Design the page around structured participation packages.

For each category, display the packages that actually exist in the
application or that have been explicitly configured.

Each package should support:
- Package name.
- Price and currency.
- Duration, where applicable.
- Included benefits.
- Eligibility or selection conditions.
- Application or subscription action.
- Payment status, if payment integration exists.

Do not invent prices, benefits, payment links, or package availability.

For designers, the previously proposed annual membership structure is:
Silver: USD 2,000
Gold: USD 5,000
Gold Premium: USD 6,000
Diamond: USD 10,000
Platinum: USD 20,000

Before displaying these as purchasable offers, inspect the existing
application and confirm how these packages are currently represented.
Preserve existing data and avoid creating duplicate packages.

Do not automatically apply designer prices to other categories.

If a category has no configured package, display a professional
"Registration of Interest" or "Apply for Participation" option.
Do not display "Tailored Fee on Enquiry" repeatedly throughout the page.

APPLICATION AND SUBSCRIPTION FLOW

Inspect the existing authentication, forms, database and payment systems.

Reuse existing components and database structures where appropriate.

The desired flow is:
1. Select a professional category.
2. Review available packages.
3. Compare benefits and terms.
4. Select a package or apply for participation.
5. Complete the required form.
6. Complete payment only if the relevant payment integration is
   properly configured.
7. Receive an appropriate confirmation.

Distinguish between a paid subscription and an application that requires
review or approval.

For models, make clear that payment or membership does not guarantee
casting, runway selection or a specific assignment.

For media, make clear that accreditation, interviews, publication,
broadcasting and access to restricted areas require the applicable
approval and agreements.

For technical and logistics partners, explain that participation
depends on the event's requirements, scope of work and contractual terms.

FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

- Ensure every category button leads to the correct destination.
- Do not create dead links or non-functional buttons.
- Preserve existing authentication and user data.
- Validate all forms.
- Provide clear success and error messages.
- Ensure mobile responsiveness and accessibility.
- Do not activate payments unless the existing payment configuration
  is valid and the necessary account is connected.
- Do not expose secret keys or sensitive configuration in frontend code.

COPYEDITING REQUIREMENTS

Review the entire participation page for:
- Grammar.
- Spelling.
- Punctuation.
- Consistent British or international English.
- Consistent terminology.
- Clear calls to action.
- Professional fashion-industry language.
- Consistent capitalisation.
- Removal of vague, repetitive and unfinished text.

Use "The One Fashion Week International" consistently.
Do not confuse the event with The One Movement International,
which is the wider platform.

IMPLEMENTATION PROCESS

First inspect the current page, routes, components, data models and
existing package structures.

Then implement the necessary changes without deleting existing data.

After implementation:
1. Test every category link.
2. Test every package button.
3. Test the forms.
4. Verify the mobile layout.
5. Check the browser console and server logs for errors.
6. Confirm that existing authentication still works.
7. Report which features work, which require configuration, and which
   require additional business decisions.

Do not claim that payment, subscriptions or database operations work
unless they have actually been tested.

The goal is to turn the existing page into a polished, coherent,
professional participation and subscription experience for
The One Fashion Week International.
