Permanent TOMI membership is free. The Founder says “WE DO NOT SELL BELONGING.” Revenue should primarily come from optional services, not compulsory membership subscriptions. Humanitarian giving remains separate.

**Why:** The Founder's Global Universe Experience mandate supersedes the earlier monthly-membership direction.

**How to apply:** The existing US$29 and US$99 monthly prices are NOT approved commercial pricing. Do not change pricing or Stripe without separate authorization. A possible ten-day trial concerns selected optional premium tools only, subject to later approval; it must not limit permanent free membership. Paid promotion must be labelled and cannot buy certification, institutional credibility or guaranteed programme participation.

Verified, eligible accounts may browse, publish ordinary community posts, follow across sectors and invite without completing a profile or opting into People. Directory listing remains voluntary. For an unlisted author, the Founder chose “Show name only on post”; do not expose their avatar, profession or profile link through that post.

**Why:** The Founder's Global Access mandate separates ordinary membership, profile completeness, discoverability and specialized approvals; the Founder explicitly confirmed the name-only exception for community posts.

**How to apply:** Never mass-enable directory visibility to repair community access. Preserve suspended-account, blocking and audience restrictions; keep drafts private and official certifications/commercial approvals separate.

Each event has its own income-generating professional participation services; participants need not buy a sponsor package. The owner sets the non-sponsor Summit speaker fee at US$10,000. Designers/stylists, models, hair stylists and makeup artists also need distinct service offers, but their fees have not been provided.

**Why:** The owner explicitly distinguishes impact, speaking, photo/video, branding, highlights, media opportunities and networking from purchasing sponsorship.

**How to apply:** Keep programme-specific services separate from annual sponsorship, monthly subscriptions and capital investment. Existing sponsors should have their written inclusions checked before an additional fee is proposed. Do not invent role prices, promise editorial coverage or assume all sponsor levels guarantee a speaking slot.

Ordinary member-to-member messaging must be free across all 17 sectors, without five-new-contacts-per-day or ten-pending-request product quotas. A first message may be delivered immediately into the recipient's requests; only the recipient can reply or accept to open further exchange. TOMI-organized professional introductions remain separate.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly reversed the earlier five/ten product limits and requested immediate first-message delivery, while retaining recipient consent and protections against automated spam.

**How to apply:** Preserve authentication, verification, short-term security controls, conversation consent, blocking, moderation and account restrictions. A sender cannot follow up repeatedly while a first message awaits acceptance. Free feed access does not authorize viewing other people's private conversations. Specialized commercial gates remain separate. Code approval does not authorize deployment.

Nationality must be supplied explicitly by the member, never inferred from IP address, device location, residence, language, name or ethnicity. Nationality and residence need distinct meanings and clear visibility controls; private account contact details must never be published automatically.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly requires voluntary identity information, data minimization and protection against accidental phone-number exposure.

**How to apply:** Reuse the existing member identity rather than creating duplicate profile tables. Do not reinterpret an ambiguous country field as nationality, copy private contacts into public profiles, or automatically opt members into directory visibility during initialization.

For members who choose their active sectors, the Founder requires one primary sector
and up to two additional active sectors per ordinary member, while all seventeen worlds
remain publicly discoverable. General interests are distinct from active sector
membership; never erase or silently truncate existing member preferences. Selecting sectors is not a prerequisite for general community participation. The Founder has server-authorized access to all seventeen sectors.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly revised the sector-access rule and requires one
identity with three active sectors, not separate registrations.

**How to apply:** Ask members to confirm their active choices rather than
automatically converting old interests. Enforce an approved active-membership
policy on the server, not only through a frontend selection limit. Future paid
privileges require separate approval; do not charge for sector changes, equate
donations with membership purchases or schedule automatic paid activation.

Members must voluntarily choose location visibility: hidden, country, or
country and city/region. Privacy-preserving defaults and editable controls are
required; do not infer location or collect GPS/home addresses.

**Why:** The Founder wants international professional discovery without exposing
private residence details.

**How to apply:** Reuse declared country/city information, keep owner inputs
separate from public projections, and respect visibility in nested profiles,
filters and caches. Free-text professional activities do not create new
official sectors.

Earlier requests to enable real monthly subscriptions do not authorize launching that model under the newer free-membership mandate.

**Why:** The Founder now explicitly separates free belonging from optional paid services and rejects the existing monthly prices as approved pricing.

**How to apply:** Wait for separate commercial and implementation approval. Never treat sandbox payments as real money or proof of launch readiness.

After Join the Movement, members should immediately reach an editable account/profile. Inviting ten people must NOT be mandatory for ordinary publication. Invitations may form part of future optional contribution goals; rewards require separate Founder approval. The earlier ten-person business-attestation rule is a distinct requirement, not silently repealed.

**Why:** The Founder's newer mandate explicitly rejects mandatory invitations for ordinary publication and requests optional meaningful contribution and recognition instead.

**How to apply:** Ordinary publication must not depend on recruitment. Keep invitation tracking and records; do not introduce rewards without separate approval. Removing the ordinary-publication invitation condition does not authorize weakening existing specialized commercial access controls, changing prices, activating trials or modifying Stripe. Prevent fake accounts and artificial engagement. Attestations remain supporting evidence, not proof of legal registration.

Videos should retain their native aspect ratio, support member comments and likes, and offer sharing to social networks. Thanks Emirates should use a UAE flag, classical animation and revised typography, featuring multiple emirates including Abu Dhabi rather than Dubai alone.

**Why:** The stakeholder supplied additional portrait videos and requested social amplification and inclusive UAE representation.

**How to apply:** Avoid forced landscape crops and respect reduced-motion settings. Distinguish user-initiated sharing from automatic posting to a connected social account.

Members should be able to list Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn profiles. The stakeholder also wants the possibility of republishing a member's own Instagram post on TOMI.

**Why:** They requested profile links and separately asked whether a post published on Instagram could also be published on the member's TOMI account.

**How to apply:** Treat a profile URL, an authorised account connection and a replicated post as separate capabilities. Preserve member choice and TOMI publishing eligibility; never describe an entered URL as a connected account or automatically copy content without authorisation.

During the current free-access phase, each member has one primary sector and up to two additional active sectors, within one TOMI identity. Public exploration of all seventeen worlds remains available.

**Why:** The consolidated Founder directive sets a three-active-sector policy and explicitly rejects separate accounts per sector.

**How to apply:** Preserve older interests independently; do not truncate them or treat an interest as joining a sector. Future changes to the active limit require Founder configuration and an explicit review of existing constraints, not an invented paid tier.

Members control whether their country and city/region are shown. Do not collect residential addresses, GPS coordinates or continuous location tracking.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly requests privacy-conscious geographic discovery and location visibility choices.

**How to apply:** Discovery must operate on permitted displayed locations, never infer a hidden place from private owner data or indirect filter results.

An authenticated TOMI account has already joined the Movement. Never require a second membership application or generic expression of interest. Keep opportunity enquiries separate from membership, and network participation consent separate from both.

**Why:** The later ecosystem architecture directive explicitly corrects duplicate membership journeys and calls for one member identity.

**How to apply:** Use My Space for signed-in members. First-time profile setup may require essential supported fields; existing members retain their account and access, with a prefilled completion invitation rather than re-registration. Individual/organization storage extensions require approval before migration. Do not create a second password or identity system.

Member discovery should use explicit saved sector choices and transparent suggestions from declared professional information, while keeping all 17 official sectors accessible. With no chosen sector, preserve general discovery. A recommendation is not a saved follow or an algorithmically personalized feed.

**Why:** The later directive authorizes relevant sector recommendations, multiple followed worlds and structured onboarding; it does not authorize automatic following or disclosure of private preferences.

**How to apply:** Save choices only after member confirmation. The seventeen sectors are ongoing communities, distinct from the six official programmes. Reuse existing resources and authorized network data; do not pretend an absent course, sector publishing system or opportunity is live. Bookmarks are not registrations, programme links are not admission, and subscription or messaging eligibility changes still need separate authorization.

The One Awards should include every officially established ambassador, with sourced biographies and official social links. Separate regional impact-leader selections (Africa, USA and eventually other regions/sectors) from TOMI ambassadors.

Do not display the “Voices of Peace” directory of Instagram handles in the sector worlds.

**Why:** The user repeated that these outbound Instagram lists must be removed. This does not request deleting official ambassador profiles or genuine campaign functionality.

**How to apply:** Do not reintroduce the handle directory when updating Fashion or other worlds; distinguish it from sourced official profiles.

Present The One Key, TOMI and shared ecosystem discovery tools before sector-world content. Visitors explicitly select a sector, then click its key to open that world; Fashion must not open automatically.

**Why:** The user wants the ecosystem explained first and the sector choice followed by an intentional key-opening action, with other pillars and detailed content afterwards.

**How to apply:** Preserve free access and direct sector URLs. A gateway selection is navigation, not a saved preference, membership application, follow or purchase.

The same peace-ambassador nominees are announced as planned Summit speakers and Fashion Week red-carpet participants, as well as participants in other activities. Use one shared profile across programmes, not separate contradictory biographies.

**Why:** The stakeholder explicitly clarified these cross-programme roles. A nomination is not an award already won, and an announcement is not proof of past attendance.

**How to apply:** Preserve nominee and planned-participation wording; source professional work and businesses separately from TOMI's own announcement.

**Why:** The stakeholder asks for privileged ambassador profiles, collaboration enquiries and six notable figures per sector, citing Mandela, Dangote and the Obamas as editorial examples.

**How to apply:** Attribute and date factual sources; Google and Wikipedia are discovery aids, not proof of TOMI affiliation. Do not imply deceased leaders are available for collaboration, invent private contacts, or label a curated selection an authoritative global ranking.
