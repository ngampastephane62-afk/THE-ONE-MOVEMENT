---
name: Community communication rules
description: Optional digests, verified-member welcome and privacy boundaries requested by the Founder.
---

Daily/weekly community email is opt-in, with in-app-only and off choices,
functional unsubscribe, suppression handling and at most one routine digest
per local platform day. No historical mass mailing, empty digest or automatic
initial activation. Founder reviews the first issue and receives an authorized
single test before scheduling is enabled.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly requires permission-based community engagement
without duplicate sends, historical notification floods or invented activity.

**How to apply:** Persist consent and delivery deduplication, use actual published
content and current discovery permissions, keep account/security messages separate,
and distinguish provider acceptance from confirmed delivery.

Verified members may access the general community without a 100%-complete profile
or sector selection. Discovery and public welcome announcements remain subject to
visibility choices; Founder registration notifications cover private accounts too.

**Why:** The Founder wants new members to participate immediately without exposing
private identities or requiring completion as an entry barrier.

**How to apply:** Treat onboarding reminders as encouragement, not an implicit
access gate. Do not expose private email/contact information in announcements.

The Founder wants notifications for new community members and incoming private
messages, with email fallback when a message recipient is absent. These
transactional alerts are distinct from the optional editorial newsletter.

**Why:** The Founder reported that the existing messaging screen gives no usable
notification of incoming activity and explicitly requested offline email alerts.

**How to apply:** Notify only authorized recipients of private messages and
mentions; never broadcast their content to the community. Community welcome
alerts must retain discovery/privacy restrictions. Avoid historical notification
floods and repeated emails for the same unread activity; provide notification
controls and verify the sender connection before enabling delivery.
