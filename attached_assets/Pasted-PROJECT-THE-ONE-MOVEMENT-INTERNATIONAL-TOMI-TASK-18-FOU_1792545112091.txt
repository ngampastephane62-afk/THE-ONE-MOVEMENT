==================================================
PUBLIC NEWS INTEGRATION
==================================================

Connect the CMS to the existing
public TOMI News page.

When the Founder publishes an article:

1. The content is saved persistently.
2. The article becomes publicly accessible.
3. It appears in the News listing.
4. Its images and videos display correctly.
5. Its public URL works.
6. The article can be shared.
7. The Founder can edit it later.

Drafts must remain private.

Changes to published articles must be
saved as separate drafts until published.

Do not duplicate the existing press archive.
