---
name: Homepage reveal principle
description: Defines the lasting information hierarchy for TOMI's public homepage and dedicated pages.
---

The homepage should follow **see → feel → understand → discover → join**. It introduces TOMI, shows visual proof, previews the ecosystem and programs, and converges on Join. Detailed explanations stay on their dedicated pages.

**Why:** The Founder explicitly rejected a long digital-brochure homepage. The public experience should progressively reveal enough to create understanding and interest without repeating every program, timeline, platform concept, or partnership detail.

**How to apply:** Keep homepage copy concise and media-led. Preserve deeper approved content on Our Vision, Ecosystem, Programs, program detail, News, Partners, Media, Join, Privacy and Terms according to each page's single purpose.
