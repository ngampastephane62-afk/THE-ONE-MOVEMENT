---
name: Public live-versus-future boundary
description: Truth standard for public claims about current services, future platform features and institutional attribution.
---

Public copy must describe the website, confirmed programs and enquiry pathways as the current experience. The AI Copilot, ASK TOMI, social network, member accounts, collaboration workspace, marketplace, wallet and payments are future direction until genuinely functional. An expression of interest never creates an account, membership or confirmed place.

**Why:** The Founder direction includes a larger AI-powered collaboration ecosystem, but launch safety requires a clear boundary between that vision and what visitors can use now. Institutional-support attribution must also remain subject to Founder confirmation unless source evidence is available.

**How to apply:** Review every new public page, CTA, press description and program announcement for present-tense access claims. Use bounded privacy language, preserve five completed Fashion Week editions with the sixth planned for November and currently postponed, and do not imply institutional endorsement without confirmation.
