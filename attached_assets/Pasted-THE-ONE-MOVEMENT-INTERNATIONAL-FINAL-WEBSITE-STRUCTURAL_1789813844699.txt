CORE FOUNDER DIRECTION
==================================================

The public TOMI website must stop behaving like a long digital brochure/PDF.

The new experience is:

SEE → FEEL → UNDERSTAND → DISCOVER → JOIN

The homepage is NOT supposed to explain everything.

Its purpose is to:
1. Introduce The One Movement.
2. Make people feel the Movement.
3. Show enough proof and ecosystem depth to create interest.
4. Lead people toward JOIN THE MOVEMENT.
5. Allow deeper information to live on dedicated pages.

Core UX principle:

REVEAL, DON'T DUMP.

Content should progressively reveal as the visitor scrolls.
