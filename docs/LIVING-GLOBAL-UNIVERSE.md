# TOMI — Living Global Universe

## Authorization

The comprehensive frontend directive supersedes the earlier Real Estate-only
pilot scope. Existing sector URLs, programmes, community, enquiry and member
routes remain the product foundation. No new backend, authentication, database,
payment, publishing permission or production operation is authorized.

## Presentation contracts

- Seventeen worlds use one configuration-driven visual layer; Real Estate retains
  its richer authored pilot. Professional roles and learning topics are editorial,
  never invented member records, courses or certifications.
- Discovery matches a local index of actual sectors, programmes and pages. It
  does not search private profiles, save interests or make external AI requests.
- TOMI AI is guided navigation with fixed responses, explicitly not an AI service.
- The One Key is a future optional concept, not a purchasable entitlement.
  Public exploration and ordinary community belonging remain free.
- Creative Space is a reviewed-contribution concept, not a public upload service.
- Connected brands keep distinct identities and commercial journeys. A navigation
  concept is not evidence of shared legal ownership.
- Editorial coverage, documented partners and sponsored placements must remain
  distinct. A coverage attribution does not create a sponsorship or endorsement.

## Future Founder Command Center

Extend the existing administration system only under separate authorization.
Prefer a structured editor of allowlisted content blocks over arbitrary HTML,
scripts or drag-and-drop executable layouts. Reuse the validation, private assets,
immutable versions, optimistic concurrency, preview and explicit publication
architecture in [17-WORLDS-ARCHITECTURE.md](17-WORLDS-ARCHITECTURE.md#6-the-one-creative-studio--architecture-proposal-only).

Suggested approved block types:

| Block | Allowed edits | Review boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World introduction | Approved text, existing motif preset, licensed asset reference | Sector identity and routes remain stable |
| Cultural chapter | Text, source, caption, approved archive reference | Historical and cultural accuracy |
| Partner placement | Existing organization reference, approved label and display period | Evidence of relationship; sponsorship never implies verification |
| Press article | Existing coverage reference, publisher credit and known original link | No false attribution or invented URL |
| Brand navigation | Approved identity and verified destination | No legal or payment-flow consolidation |
| Creative contribution | Approved submission reference and attribution | Consent, rights and editorial acceptance before publication |

Separate edit, review and publication capabilities must be approved and enforced
server-side in a future phase, not inferred from a frontend button. Preview must
not expose drafts; publishing content must not deploy application code.

## Verification boundary

Isolated component/event checks and TypeScript/build checks cannot establish real
device sharing, clipboard permission behavior, authenticated access, cultural asset
licensing beyond the existing archive, production availability or release readiness.
No production readiness claim or deployment is part of this delivery.
