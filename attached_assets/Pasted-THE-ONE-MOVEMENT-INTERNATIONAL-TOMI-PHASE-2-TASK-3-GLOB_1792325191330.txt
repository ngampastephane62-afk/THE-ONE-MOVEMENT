- Community development

- Opportunities

"YOU DON'T JUST JOIN A PLATFORM.

2. FUNDAMENTAL MEMBERSHIP PRINCIPLE
==================================================

WE DO NOT SELL BELONGING.

Joining TOMI should be free and accessible.

Members should be able to:

- Create their identity
- Discover the ecosystem
- Explore the 17 sectors
- Connect with people
- Participate in communities
- Access public knowledge
- Discover opportunities
- Contribute to the movement

Membership must not require a monthly payment.

Members must retain control over their
accounts and their ability to leave.

The previously discussed 10-day trial should
apply only to selected optional premium tools,
if approved later.

It must not limit permanent free membership.

3. ECONOMIC MODEL
==================================================

The Founder wants TOMI to generate revenue
primarily through valuable optional services,
not compulsory membership subscriptions.

Investigate a future model based on:

- Promotional campaigns
- Content boosting
- Professional visibility
- Business promotion
- Media coverage
- Advertising
- Featured opportunities
- Event participation
- Exhibitions
- Marketplace services
- Professional services
- Optional premium tools
- Sponsorship agreements

Members should pay for specific additional
services when they need them.

Example:

A fashion designer joins TOMI for free.

They build their profile and connect with
other members.

When launching a collection, they may
purchase an optional promotional campaign.

A business may similarly pay to promote
a product or service.

IMPORTANT:

Paid promotion must not automatically grant
official certification, institutional credibility,
or guaranteed participation in official programs.

Sponsored visibility must be clearly identified.

The existing $29 and $99 monthly prices
are NOT approved commercial pricing.

Do not change pricing or Stripe.
