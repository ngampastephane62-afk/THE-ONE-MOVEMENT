OBJECTIVE 3 — PROPOSE THE FOUNDER WORKSPACE
==================================================

Design a practical private Founder Workspace
accessible after login from the Founder's own account.

PROPOSED MODULES:

1. OVERVIEW

Website status, content overview, recent updates,
drafts and publication activity.

2. PAGE MANAGER

Edit approved text sections and content blocks
on existing website pages.

3. NEWS & ARTICLES

Create, edit, preview, publish, unpublish
and archive articles.

4. PARTNERS & SPONSORS

Add partner names, logos, descriptions,
website links and display order.

5. MEDIA LIBRARY

Manage approved images, videos, banners
and visual assets.

6. PROGRAMS & EVENTS

Update descriptions, dates, images and
approved event information.

7. WEBSITE BRANDING

Manage approved logo placements,
selected banners and visual assets.

8. CONTENT REVIEW

Preview changes before publishing.

9. PUBLISHING HISTORY

Record who changed what and when.

10. FOUNDER SETTINGS

Manage Founder-specific workspace preferences
without exposing private credentials.

IMPORTANT:

The Founder Workspace must use a premium,
modern and practical interface consistent
with TOMI's existing visual identity.

It must be easy to use on desktop and mobile.
